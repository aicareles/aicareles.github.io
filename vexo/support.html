Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create AI subtitles?

Import your video, select the AI subtitle feature, and Vexo will generate captions automatically. You can edit subtitle text, style, and timing before exporting.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. Vexo processes your videos locally on your device. Your videos, photos, and audio files are not uploaded or stored by us.

How do I remove ads or unlock premium features?

You can purchase Vexo Premium inside the app to unlock additional features and remove advertisements.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Vexo, go to the Premium page, and select "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

Which video formats are supported?

Vexo supports common video formats including MP4, MOV, MKV, and other popular formats depending on your device.
