FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Vexo?

Vexo is an AI subtitle editor and video toolkit for Android. It combines automatic subtitles, subtitle translation and practical video tools in one app.

Can Vexo automatically generate subtitles?

Yes. Vexo can use AI to generate subtitles from spoken content in your videos.

Can I translate subtitles with Vexo?

Yes. Vexo supports subtitle translation and multiple languages, making it easier to create videos for different audiences.

Does Vexo include video editing tools?

Yes. Vexo includes practical video tools such as editing, compression, format conversion and video cover changes.

What devices support Vexo?

Vexo is designed for Android phones and tablets. Availability may depend on your Android version and device capabilities.
