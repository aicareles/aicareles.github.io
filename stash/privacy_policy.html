Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

Thank you for using Stash. Your privacy matters to us — which is why Stash was designed around a simple principle: your content stays on your device.

Stash is a private inbox for the things you want to keep. Save links, web articles, images, videos, audio, and documents from the Share sheet or your clipboard, then organize, read, and revisit them whenever you like.

No Data Collection

Stash does not collect any personal information. There is no account, no registration, and no sign‑in. We do not track how you use the app, and we do not operate analytics, advertising, crash‑reporting, or any other data‑collection services. Nothing about you or your activity is ever sent to us.

Your Content Stays on Your Device

Everything you save — links, articles, photos, videos, and other files — is stored only in Stash's private, on‑device storage. Your collection is not uploaded to any server and is never accessible to us or to any third party. You are always in control of your data, and you can delete individual items or everything at any time.

When you use the reader to save an article for offline reading, the extracted copy is also stored locally on your device.

Network Access to Websites You Choose

When you save a web link, Stash needs to retrieve the page from that website's own servers (directly from your device) to build a preview and an offline copy. This request goes to the website you chose to save, not to us. That website may record your visit according to its own policies, just as it would if you opened the page in a browser.

We recommend saving content only from websites you trust, and reviewing any website's privacy practices before you save from it.

Clipboard

If you copy a link and return to Stash, we may offer to save it for you. This is done only on your device and only at your request. To avoid asking you repeatedly about the same link, Stash keeps a short, local record of links you have already been offered. This record never leaves your device.

Backups and Sharing Are Yours

Any export, backup, or sharing of your saved content is initiated by you, from your device, using the standard Share features of your operating system. Stash itself never sends your content anywhere.

Data Deletion

You can delete any item, group of items, or your entire collection at any time within Stash. Deleting the app from your device removes all locally stored data permanently. To keep a copy before deleting, you can export your data to a file of your own.

Children's Privacy

Stash does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, no special permission is required from or on behalf of children to use Stash.

Security

Because your data never leaves your device, it is protected by the same security that protects your phone. We recommend using a passcode or biometric lock on your device to keep your collection private.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change the way Stash handles your data, we will update this page and note the date of the change. Because Stash does not collect your data today, you can rely on this page to stay informed of any future changes.
