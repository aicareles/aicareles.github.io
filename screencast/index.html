FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Screen Cast?

Screen Cast is an Android casting app that lets you stream videos, photos, music and other supported media from your phone to compatible TVs and devices.

Can I cast videos from Android to TV?

Yes. Screen Cast can stream supported videos from your Android device to compatible TVs and receivers.

Does Screen Cast support DLNA?

Yes. Screen Cast supports DLNA casting to compatible devices connected to the same local network.

Does Screen Cast support Chromecast?

Screen Cast supports casting to compatible Chromecast and Google Cast devices for supported media.

Do my phone and TV need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

For network-based casting such as DLNA, your Android device and receiving device generally need to be connected to the same local network.
