Frequently Asked Questions

Does Cast Receiver require root access or complex setup? +
No. Simply install Cast Receiver on your Android device or Android TV, ensure both devices are connected to the same Wi-Fi network, and start the app. It will instantly appear on your sender device.
Can I cast content from YouTube or Netflix? +
Cast Receiver supports general AirPlay video streaming and DLNA DMR casting. DRM-protected content (like Netflix) may have restrictions depending on client-side security policies.
Why isn't my iPhone finding the TV? +
Please verify that both your iPhone and Android device are on the exact same Wi-Fi router network and that "AP Isolation" (Guest Mode) is disabled in your router settings.
