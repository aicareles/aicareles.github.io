FAQ

Does it need root access or manual setup?

No. Install Cast Receiver on your Android phone, tablet, or TV, connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network, and open the app. It appears automatically in AirPlay and DLNA device lists.

Can I cast Netflix or other DRM-protected apps?

Standard AirPlay video, screen mirroring, and DLNA streams work normally. DRM-protected services such as Netflix restrict third-party receivers and may show a black screen or refuse playback.

Why can’t my iPhone or PC see the TV?

Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network) and that router settings like “AP Isolation” or “Client Isolation” are turned off, as they block local mDNS and SSDP discovery.
