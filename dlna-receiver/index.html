FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is DLNA Receiver for Android TV?

DLNA Receiver is a dedicated DLNA and UPnP Digital Media Renderer (DMR) app developed by Leox Studio for Android TV, Google TV, TV boxes, and projectors. Once installed, your TV appears as a DLNA playback target on your local Wi-Fi network so you can push videos, music, and photos directly to the big screen.

Which devices and apps can cast to DLNA Receiver?

Any standard DLNA or UPnP sender on the same Wi-Fi network works with DLNA Receiver, including Android phones and tablets (using Screen Cast, BubbleUPnP, Web Video Caster, or built-in gallery casting), Windows PCs (Cast to Device / Windows Media Player), NAS media servers (Synology, QNAP, Plex, Jellyfin), and Linux media players.

What is the difference between DLNA Receiver and Cast Receiver?

DLNA Receiver is a lightweight, dedicated DLNA/UPnP-only receiver ideal for Android, Windows, and NAS users. Cast Receiver – DLNA & AirPlay is an all-in-one receiver that supports both AirPlay (for iPhone, iPad, and Mac) and DLNA/UPnP in a single app.

Is DLNA Receiver free, or does it offer a one-time lifetime purchase?

DLNA Receiver is free to download and try on Google Play. For full premium features, you can choose between a flexible subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase with no recurring fees.

Does DLNA Receiver work without an internet connection?

Yes. All DLNA and UPnP streaming happens directly over your local Wi-Fi or Ethernet router. Your media files never leave your home network and are not uploaded to any cloud server.

Why can't my phone or PC find DLNA Receiver on my TV?

Make sure both your Android TV and sender device are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network), disable AP/client isolation on your router, and confirm that DLNA Receiver is open and running on your TV.
