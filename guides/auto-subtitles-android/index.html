Pro Tips for Cleaner Captions & Audio

Clean noisy voiceovers first

Background hum lowers speech-to-text accuracy. Pair Vexo with AudioX to apply AI noise reduction, 5-band EQ, or vocal cleanup before subtitling.

Keep captions in the safe zone

Place subtitles slightly above the bottom third so TikTok, Reels, and Shorts UI buttons never cover your text.

Short lines boost watch time

Break long sentences into 1–2 short lines per beat so viewers can read effortlessly on small screens.

Save references & hooks

Collect inspiration links and script ideas from any social app using Stash so your next video ideas stay organized.
