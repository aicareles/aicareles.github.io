3-Step Setup on Android TV & Google TV

1

Install an AirPlay receiver on your TV

Open the Google Play Store on your Android TV, Google TV, TV box, or projector and install Cast Receiver (if you also want DLNA for Android/Windows/NAS) or AirPlay Receiver (if you only use Apple devices).

2

Connect TV and Apple device to the same Wi-Fi

Confirm your TV and iPhone, iPad, or Mac are on the same local Wi-Fi network (avoid Guest Wi-Fi). Launch the receiver app on your TV and note the device name shown on screen.

3

Start Screen Mirroring or AirPlay

On iPhone/iPad, swipe down from the top-right corner to open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, and select your TV. On Mac, click Control Center in the menu bar → Screen Mirroring (or click the AirPlay icon in QuickTime / Safari / Music).

AirPlay Not Showing Android TV? 4 Quick Fixes

1. Turn off VPNs temporarily

Active VPNs on iPhone, Mac, or Android TV route traffic away from local mDNS discovery. Pause the VPN while casting.

2. Check Guest Wi-Fi & AP Isolation

Make sure neither device is on a Guest SSID. On router settings, disable "AP Isolation" or "Client Isolation" so devices can see each other.

3. Allow Local Network permission on iOS

On iPhone/iPad, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and ensure the app you are casting from has Local Network enabled.

4. Use 5 GHz Wi-Fi or Ethernet for TV

For smoother 1080p/4K video and lower screen-mirroring latency, connect your Android TV via Ethernet or 5 GHz Wi-Fi.
