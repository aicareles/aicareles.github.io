AirPlay Receiver on Android

Features

What you can send

Use AirPlay from an Apple device and receive it on Android.

Video Streaming

Play video from an iPhone, iPad, or Mac on your Android screen.

Audio Streaming

Send music and podcasts from an Apple device to Android.

Screen Mirroring

Show your iPhone or iPad screen on your Android device.

Wi-Fi connection

Keep both devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

Photo Receiver

View photos from your iPhone or Mac on your Android screen.

Local Network Only

AirPlay streams over your local network; it does not need a cloud connection.

How it works

Set it up in three steps

1

Install the app

Download AirPlay Receiver from Google Play and open it. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network.

2

Choose AirPlay Receiver

Open the AirPlay menu on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac, then select AirPlay Receiver.

3

Start streaming

Play a song or video, or start screen mirroring. It will appear on your Android device.
